## Future Journey rule

**ONE JOURNEY = ONE PRIMARY CULTURAL QUESTION**

A future Journey may add **1 primary question + 1–2 secondary knowledge anchors**. No single Journey should attempt to become a complete Forbidden City encyclopedia.
