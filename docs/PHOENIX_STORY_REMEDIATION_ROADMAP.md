## 2. Audit method

Each Story was reviewed for:

- current Story shape;
- protagonist humanity;
- causal relationship;
- human stakes;
- conflict;
- choice and cost;
- climax quality;
- Story Memory Moment;
- behavioral ending;
- place pressure;
- Chinese narrative quality;
- exposition risk;
- human-reader template risk;
- nearest Story collision.

Founder-approved Stories are not modified by this audit. `REOPEN RECOMMENDED` is a request for Founder authorization, not permission to edit.
