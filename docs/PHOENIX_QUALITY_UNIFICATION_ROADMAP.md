# Phoenix Quality Unification Roadmap

**System:** Phoenix Product Standard System v1.0  
**Status:** BINDING ORDER OF OPERATIONS  
**Stable baseline:** PR `#137`, Commit `5fcadcb4a1c424706957e9d6bd72cc7f9f2c6977`

## 1. Roadmap law

All work follows `NEW RESULT >= CURRENT STABLE BASELINE`. A later stage MUST NOT begin while an earlier binding dependency is unresolved, blocked, rejected, or missing Founder approval.

This roadmap does not itself authorize implementation.

## 2. Foundation sequence

1. Maintain one authoritative Stable PR and Stable Commit.
2. Complete governance standards and acceptance matrices.
3. Complete the read-only Full Application Audit and deduplicated issue ledger.
4. Repair P0 immediately if identified.
5. Finish routing, access, and critical-persistence P1 repairs.
6. Reverify stable comparison and failure recovery.

## 3. Narrative repair sequence before broad content expansion

The required order is:

1. Finish routing, access, and critical-persistence P1 repairs.
2. Merge Phoenix Narrative and Discovery Standard after Founder review.
3. Founder confirms the content model, including normal protagonist mode, special protagonist mode, Reflection / Writing structure, account scope where relevant, and pilot order.
4. Complete one normal Story pilot, recommended `beijing-summer-palace`.
5. Obtain Founder mobile approval for that exact normal pilot Commit and Preview.
6. Complete one special Story pilot, recommended `tide-letter`.
7. Obtain Founder mobile approval for that exact special pilot Commit and Preview.
8. Expand only in controlled batches of two to three Journeys.
9. Re-run library differentiation after every batch.
10. Do not start new Journey expansion until existing pilot quality is proven.

A rejected pilot returns to revision. It does not authorize a different pilot or mass rewriting.

## 4. Explicit prohibitions

The roadmap prohibits:

- rewriting 27 normal Journeys in one task;
- rewriting nine special Journeys in one task;
- using one shared Story template;
- approving a batch only through aggregate score;
- starting a second pilot before the first is decided;
- inferring a reusable template from the first pilot draft;
- batch Story rewrite before pilot approval;
- combining narrative repair with routing, access, persistence, visual replacement, or new Journey expansion unless separately authorized;
- accepting `360 / 360 PASS`, `score 100`, `average 100`, or `all fields present` as literary approval.

Violation requires `BATCH_EXPANSION_BEFORE_PILOT_APPROVAL` or another applicable blocking code.

## 5. Controlled content waves

After both pilots are approved:

- each batch contains two to three Journeys by default;
- each Journey retains an independent Story / Discovery Design Matrix and acceptance record;
- automated structural and human literary results remain separate;
- opening, ending, narrative engine, protagonist, Relationship, Choice, Consequence, cultural anchor, Story / Discovery separation, level invariants, and memory anchor are rechecked;
- lower quality at scale blocks the next batch;
- visual and rights changes follow their own approval gates.

## 6. Remaining quality sequence

After product-model and narrative pilots:

1. learning-flow and stage repair;
2. vocabulary provenance and reviewed-example integrity;
3. dedicated visual identity and badge repair;
4. privacy, Secret, processor, retention, and deletion closure;
5. rights and provenance closure;
6. mobile, narration, Shadowing, accessibility, and runtime visual evidence;
7. final regression audit;
8. Founder mobile approval;
9. Ready or merge only after explicit authorization.
