# Phoenix Journey Content Expansion Roadmap

Status: FOUNDER STRATEGIC ROADMAP — NON-BINDING QUALITY AUTHORITY

CURRENT FOUNDER STRATEGIC ROADMAP

This roadmap records the current Founder-declared sequencing for content expansion. It is a non-binding strategic ordering and does not alter canonical quality standards or acceptance gates.

PHASE A
NATIONAL SEED COVERAGE

- Establish baseline Gold coverage across province-level regions and major cities.
- Prioritize cultural and human depth over raw story counts.
- Objective: create seed Journeys that enable future Place Story Universe expansion.

PHASE B
BEIJING DEPTH PROGRAM

- Focused City Place Network expansion for Beijing.
- Support multiple independent Journeys per Place only when Same-Place differentiation and Incremental Value are demonstrated.

PHASE C
SHANGHAI DEPTH PROGRAM

- Execute only after Founder declares BEIJING DEPTH SUFFICIENT.

PHASE D
OTHER FOUNDER-AUTHORIZED PROVINCES / CITIES

- Sequential Founder authorization for subsequent provinces and cities.

Additional principles

- It is permissible in future phases to return to Beijing, Shanghai, or any existing Place to add genuinely new independent Journeys when justified by Incremental Cultural Value and Incremental Human Value.
- The Founder-declared pilot or method for a Story (for example, Summer Palace method evidence) IS NOT a Story template, Level template, Cultural template, or Place Story Universe template.
- "《留下痕迹的风景》" is a Founder-approved Gold Story and Pilot evidence set, and must not be treated as a future template for Yiheyuan or other Place Stories.
- Roadmap status = NON-BINDING FOUNDER STRATEGY. FOUNDER APPROVAL = PENDING

Notes:

- Roadmap must not be used to justify coverage quotas that reduce Gold quality.

LONG-TERM STORY UNIVERSE CAPABILITY

After broad first-Gold coverage exists and Founder separately authorizes a mature Place for additional depth, Phoenix may expand that Place through multiple complete Gold Story Experiences rather than one encyclopedic Story.

Future Story Universe exploration may vary by:

- Subject: human, artifact, craft, place, community/institution, ecology, idea/practice;
- verified Time Layer;
- Human Lens;
- Historical Scale;
- Truth Mode;
- Cultural Slice;
- Primary Story Depth;
- Human Need;
- Narrative Engine.

The roadmap does not require one Story for every category, era, depth dimension, or historical family. `ONE STORY SHOULD NOT EXHAUST THE PLACE`, and unused valid cultural/historical material may remain a `FUTURE PLACE STORY OPPORTUNITY`.

Historical expansion principles:

- Historical truth always overrides narrative convenience.
- Historical people, events, artifact provenance, dates, institutions, practices, and place connections must remain source-grounded.
- Unknown or contested history must remain unknown or contested rather than being completed by plausible invention.
- Artifact/Object Biography, historical-person Story, craft/maker Story, Place-through-time Story, memory/loss/recovery Story, and Place Network Story are future descriptive possibilities, not production quotas or templates.
- Historical depth must enter through human action, object life, or place change rather than a chronology dump.

PLACE STORY UNIVERSE EXPERIENCE PILOT ORDER

The future target order is:

1. continue one-Journey-at-a-time Gold development under Founder authorization;
2. build a sufficiently broad first-Gold baseline before prioritizing same-Place multiplicity;
3. choose one already-mature Founder-approved Gold Place;
4. create exactly one same-Place second-Story pilot under separate Founder authorization;
5. prove independent Human Value, Cultural Value, Story Depth Profile, Story Signature, historical truth where applicable, Same-Place Anti-Template PASS, and whole-library differentiation;
6. obtain Founder approval for the second complete Gold Story Experience;
7. only then consider a separately authorized multi-Story runtime pilot;
8. runtime pilot should begin minimally with `UNSEEN FIRST`, `STABLE WITHIN THE RUN`, and progress-aware behavior rather than pure random Story selection.

This roadmap does not authorize Story Experience runtime, persistence, progress UI, data-model migration, or a new same-Place Story by itself.

STORY DEPTH ROUND 2

After several real Founder-reviewed Journeys use the Story Depth + Historical Truth architecture, perform `PHOENIX STORY DEPTH MODEL · ROUND 2`.

Round 2 should review which depth dimensions materially improved Story quality, which overlap, which are too theoretical, which should become stronger requirements, which should stay optional, and which new gaps become visible through actual Story work.

`DEPTH OPPORTUNITY != GOLD DEFECT`. Round 2 exists to improve the model from evidence, not to reopen every already-complete Gold Story.
