**Causal distinction**

The Chengdu engine depends on repeated temporary relinquishment and return of a shared spatial claim. The decisive proof is not Lin Xia's private understanding but another participant independently reproducing the yield-and-return behavior. The protocol exists only after repetition transfers operational participation across people.

**Structural importance**

**Journey-specific naming check**
