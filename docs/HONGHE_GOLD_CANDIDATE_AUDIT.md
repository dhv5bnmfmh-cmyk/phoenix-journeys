## Story Identity

Primary depth: **公共职责与私人互惠不可同时保全时的关系代价**.

Luo Qiu is a fictional contemporary terrace farmer making her first ditch round as a community water keeper. Her longtime neighbor Ma Lan has privately promised a buffalo to plough Luo Qiu's last terrace after Luo Qiu helped repair Ma Lan's bund. Luo Qiu discovers that the wooden groove directing water toward Ma Lan's field was widened overnight, thinning the other branch.

Ma Lan asks Luo Qiu to leave the extra share until noon and reminds her of the buffalo. Luo Qiu restores the groove to the dimensions agreed by water users. The lower branch rises again. Ma Lan leaves with the buffalo. Luo Qiu's own final terrace remains unploughed that day and the relationship is not verbally repaired.

Memory Moment: the recut hardwood divider seats into the water; two flows separate as the buffalo bell turns away.

Ending: Luo Qiu works the bund of her own unploughed terrace by foot while both restored channels continue sounding. No explanatory reconciliation sentence is added.
