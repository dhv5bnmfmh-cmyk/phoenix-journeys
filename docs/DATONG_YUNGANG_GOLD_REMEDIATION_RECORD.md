## Verified-world mechanism

The Story uses a sourced configuration, not a claim of uniqueness in all history: Yungang’s capital-backed colossal construction context changed after the 494 move, while smaller carving continued at the old capital site. A fictional family can plausibly encounter that change. No source is required to prove that this invented family existed; sources are required for the world they encounter.

Removal: without the transition in patronage and scale, the old long measuring line, the exclusive succession conflict, the three-way division and the smaller-stone consequence lose their current causal reason. Longmen replacement reverses the pressure: after 494 Longmen became a new imperial center. The current architecture cannot move there unchanged.

## De-skinned comparison

An appointed heir wants two dependents to become agents before a separating departure. The authority figure offers exclusive status. The heir irreversibly divides the object that concentrates authority, loses the title, and watches the former dependents act independently. The authority figure looks back but does not reconcile.

This is not Longmen’s coauthors deleting unsupported work, Kaiping’s siblings renegotiating a contribution, a task/redesign loop, an old-object reunion, or a deadline refusal. Machine Rule A / Rule B and human de-skinned review both pass.

Historical context remains taught-before-tested at the same level, but the answer depends on Chinese structure rather than historical trivia. No other Journey Challenge content is remediated in PR #186.
