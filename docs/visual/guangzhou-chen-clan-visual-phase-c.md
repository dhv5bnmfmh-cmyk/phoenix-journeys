## Visual DNA

- Journey/location: 中国 → 广东省 → 广州市 → 陈家祠; present-day visitor environment inside a protected late-Qing Lingnan architectural complex.
- Story identity: a young maker discovers that fidelity across materials requires translation rather than literal copying.
- Visual narrative identity: historic material complexity → close observation → structural connection → transformed representation.
- Narrative mood: observant, tactile, precise, quietly inventive, material-aware, human-scale; never royal, ceremonial, fantastical, or tourism-spectacle driven.
- Geographic identity: enclosed Guangzhou/Lingnan courtyard world, humid subtropical atmosphere, layered halls/corridors/courtyards rather than skyline or open landscape.
- Architectural identity: verified gray-brick walls, gray tiled roofs, timber halls/corridors, stone elements, courtyards, Qingyun-lane/long-corridor depth, and richly integrated Lingnan architectural decoration. Ceramic/sculptural roof-ridge ornament may appear only in plausible placement and restrained scale.
- Materials: weathered gray brick, aged timber, mineral stone, ceramic roof sculpture, lime/plaster sculpture, painted architectural detail, metalwork where credible, and natural off-white paper as the contemporary Story-linked material.
- Season: present-day warm/humid Guangzhou; no invented seasonal spectacle.
- Weather logic: humid soft daylight; optional light post-rain condition only when wetness, reflections, clothing, sky, and surface darkening remain physically consistent.
- Time of day: morning or late morning, diffused daylight.
- Lighting: soft directional courtyard daylight and bounce light; gentle shadow transitions; enough raking light to reveal relief depth without HDR crunch, god rays, sunset orange, or fake glow.
- Color philosophy: restrained mineral palette of weathered gray brick, warm aged timber, muted stone, off-white paper, subtle vegetation green, and small pockets of authentic crafted ceramic/paint color. Avoid imperial-red dominance, gold overload, teal-orange grading, neon, and saturated postcard skies.
- Human presence: environment primary. If present, Liang Yao and He Zhen are contemporary young-adult maker peers at small-to-medium environmental scale, naturally observing or handling their own study materials, not facing camera and never staged as mentor/student.
- Clothing: understated contemporary student clothing without logos, costume, historical reenactment, or fashion-editorial styling.
- Transport: none in the heritage interior pilot world.
- Objects: only plausible visitor/maker items such as sketchbook, own paper prototype, pencil/material pouch. No paper may touch, trace, rub, attach to, or be cut against heritage fabric.
- Foreground: threshold/door/brick edge, shadowed architectural frame, or own paper prototype.
- Midground: primary verified Chen Clan architectural/craft subject and, where needed, small human action.
- Background: secondary courtyard/roof/corridor layer in humid soft atmosphere.
- Camera language: human eye-level, medium environmental distance, slightly oblique courtyard views, framed architectural depth, and close material-to-space relationships. No drone, fisheye, extreme ultra-wide, repetitive palace symmetry, or empty monumental plaza.
- Depth: deliberate foreground/midground/background separation with material hierarchy; not every decorative surface equally sharp.
- Atmosphere: quiet contemporary museum observation, humid daylight, tactile surfaces, controlled visitor density.
- Mobile readable region: every candidate must reserve a calm top zone, story-text region, and lower action region using soft gray wall, shadowed brick, muted sky fragment, low-detail floor/stone plane, or blurred courtyard depth. Dense carving cannot occupy every UI zone.
- Memory Anchor relationship: `纸桥` appears only as a plausible single-sheet cut-paper study with narrow structural connections absent from the source appearance but necessary for the paper object. Never a giant symbolic/floating bridge.
- Forbidden generic motifs: generic Chinese temple, red-lantern street, random dragons, random Buddhist symbols, northern imperial roofs, Forbidden-City-style gate axis, pagoda, fantasy Lingnan, historical costumes, tourism-poster composition, giant symbolic paper, fake Chinese text, logos, captions, watermarks.
