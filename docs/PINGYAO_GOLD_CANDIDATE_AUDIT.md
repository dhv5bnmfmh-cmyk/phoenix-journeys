### Source ledger

   Supports the integrated county-town fabric and Pingyao's role as a major financial centre in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, including banking buildings as a heritage attribute.

   Current-main verified local-government source used to cross-check the place and local cultural context.

3. 国家保密局互联网门户网站, **晋商票号：防伪保密制度的创制者**  
   Supports the draft-bank business of interregional remittance/deposit/lending; the change from moving physical silver to settling through a draft; Rishengchang in Pingyao in 1823; verification, branch payment, and anti-counterfeit mechanisms.

### Verified world model

- Pingyao is a preserved county-level city whose urban fabric includes walls, streets and lanes, shops, dwellings and temples.
- In the nineteenth to early twentieth century it was a major financial centre.
- Draft banks (`票号`) operated interregional remittance.
- A draft could function as a credit instrument so the original physical silver did not need to make the same long-distance journey.
- Destination branch verification and payment are part of the documented mechanism.
- Historical ticket-bank operations are not described as zero-risk, government guaranteed, or frictionless.

### Fiction boundary

Allowed fiction:
- 程砚、程岳、their mother, fictional cloth shop, due Beijing supplier payment.
- Private dialogue, illness, choices, disagreement, separation of accounts and emotional consequences.

Blocked:
- private motives/dialogue/actions for identifiable real historical people;
- invented law or government guarantee;
- claim that every draft bank followed every detail identically;
- claim that remittance removed all risk.

REAL PERSON PROTECTION = PASS. The Story uses no identifiable real historical person.
