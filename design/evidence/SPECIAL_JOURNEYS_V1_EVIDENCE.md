## Ten-scene responsibility

Each Journey contains exactly ten independently registered files: threshold fallback, three Story phases, Vocabulary, Discovery, Reflection, Writing, Memory and Completion. Scene-specific geometry changes focal objects, spatial balance and narrative duty; files are not color, crop, mirror or brightness variants. Text and controls retain the upper/central safe field and lower navigation clearance. No scene reveals a Challenge answer or copies Story text.

## Gate record
