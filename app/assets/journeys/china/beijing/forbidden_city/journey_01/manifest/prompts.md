# Forbidden City Journey 01 — production prompts

Prompt family: `fc01-v1`. Scene D verified revision: `fc01-d-v2`.

Every prompt uses the same visual contract: cinematic documentary illustration,
architecturally grounded real space, subtle painterly finish, quiet narrative
atmosphere, human scale, UI-safe negative space, no readable signage, no route
graphics, no fantasy, no logos, and no watermark. Portrait masters are native
9:16 recompositions, never landscape crops.

## FC01-A — Entry

Historically grounded entry through the Meridian Gate spatial sequence in early
morning light. Make the strong north–south depth and first impression of the
central route immediately legible. Architecture and space are the protagonists;
people, if present, are tiny scale references. Preserve low-detail overlay zones
without covering the principal entrance or roof silhouette.

## FC01-B — Axis

Historically grounded sequence of imperial gates and courtyards along the
Forbidden City central axis. Emphasize repeated thresholds, courtyard depth,
architectural order, and spatial hierarchy without combining unrelated palaces
into a collage. Use calm natural daylight and keep overlay zones low-detail.

## FC01-C — East route

Historically grounded courtyard where central ordering remains visible while a
real side corridor, gate, or connected court permits an eastward turn. Express
route divergence only through physical architecture and perspective. No arrows,
maps, HUD, colored route lines, or impossible connections.

## FC01-D — Shared node (`fc01-d-v2`)

Late-afternoon forecourt immediately south of Qianqingmen. Show a five-bay gate
with single-eave hip-and-gable roof, elevated pale-stone base, balustrades and
stair approaches. Preserve a legible central-axis approach and believable
lateral connection so two task routes can share one spatial fact. Use warm,
restrained closure. Landscape reserves a darker left-lower desktop safe zone;
portrait is a native 9:16 re-composition with calm lower 40% mobile safe space.
No readable plaque, fake Chinese text, distorted roof, bent pillars, duplicated
railings, impossible stairs, modern objects, tourism posing, logos, or watermark.
